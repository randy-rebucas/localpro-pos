# 9. Reports & Analytics

**Available to:** Manager, Admin, Owner

## Accessing Reports

1. Navigate to **Reports** from the sidebar
2. Select the report type from the tabs

## Available Reports

### Sales Report

Comprehensive sales overview with filtering:

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Gross Sales** | Total revenue before discounts |
| **Net Sales** | Revenue after discounts and refunds |
| **Discount Total** | Total discounts given |
| **Refund Total** | Total refunds issued |
| **Transaction Count** | Number of sales |
| **Average Order Value** | Mean sale amount |

**Filters:**
- Date range (Today, This Week, This Month, Custom)
- Branch
- Cashier
- Payment method

**Chart:** Visual sales trend line by day, week, or month.

### Product Performance Report

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Top Sellers** | Products ranked by units sold |
| **Revenue by Product** | Products ranked by revenue generated |
| **Low Performers** | Products with fewest sales |
| **Category Breakdown** | Sales distribution by category |

### VAT Report

BIR-compliant tax report:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **VATable Sales** | Total sales subject to 12% VAT |
| **VAT Amount** | 12% of VATable sales |
| **VAT-Exempt Sales** | Sales exempt from VAT |
| **Zero-Rated Sales** | Sales at 0% VAT rate |
| **Total Sales** | Grand total |

### Profit & Loss (P&L) Report

| Section | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Revenue** | Total net sales |
| **Cost of Goods Sold** | Total cost of products sold |
| **Gross Profit** | Revenue minus COGS |
| **Expenses** | Business expenses entered in the Expenses module |
| **Net Profit** | Gross profit minus expenses |

### Cash Drawer Report

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Opening Balance** | Cash at start of session |
| **Cash Sales** | Cash received from sales |
| **Cash Refunds** | Cash given for refunds |
| **Expected Balance** | Calculated amount that should be in drawer |
| **Actual Count** | Entered by cashier at closing |
| **Discrepancy** | Difference (overage or shortage) |

### Sales Journal

Detailed line-by-line record of every transaction, shown 20 rows at a time. Use **Previous** and **Next** to move between pages. The totals and the CSV, Excel and PDF exports always cover the whole date range, not just the page on screen.

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Date/Time** | Transaction timestamp |
| **Receipt #** | Serial receipt number |
| **Customer** | Customer name (if linked) |
| **Items** | Product names and quantities |
| **Subtotal** | Pre-tax amount |
| **VAT** | Tax amount |
| **Discount** | Discount applied |
| **Total** | Final amount |
| **Payment** | Payment method |
| **Cashier** | Staff who processed |

### Bundle Performance

For stores using product bundles:
- Bundle sales count
- Revenue per bundle
- Component product tracking
- Comparison to individual product sales

## Exporting Reports

All reports can be exported:

1. Apply your desired filters
2. Click **Export**
3. Choose format:

| Format | Best For |
|--------|----------|
| **CSV** | Spreadsheet analysis, importing to other systems |
| **Excel** | Formatted reports with formatting preserved |
| **PDF** | Printing, archiving, sharing with accountant |

4. The file downloads to your device

## X-Reading and Z-Reading

Both have their own tab on the Reports page. A business day runs from midnight to midnight in the store's timezone, set under **Settings**. The default is Manila time.

**X-Reading:** a sales summary for one business day. Pick the day with the **Date** field. It does not reset or lock any totals, so you can run it at any time.

**Z-Reading:** the official end-of-day report.

1. Open the **Z-Reading** tab
2. Select **Generate Z-Reading for Today**
3. Confirm when asked

Generating one locks in the day's totals against the Grand Total, and only one can be generated per business day. The tab lists the 30 latest readings, so it has no date filter.

If automation is enabled, a Z-Reading is created for you shortly after midnight in the store's timezone, covering the day that just ended. It shows **Automatic** under **Generated By**. A day that already has a reading keeps it, and a day with no sales, voids or refunds gets none.

## Scheduled Reports

If automation is enabled, reports can be sent automatically:
- Daily sales report sent by email or SMS shortly after midnight, once the day's Z-Reading is ready
- Weekly performance report sent Monday morning
- Monthly P&L sent on the 1st of each month

To choose who gets the daily report, go to **Settings > Notifications > Daily Sales Report** and enter an email address, a mobile number, or both. Mobile numbers use the international format, such as +639171234567. Leave both blank to stop it. If no report email is entered, the report goes to the business email when email notifications are on.

The email shows gross and net sales, refunds, discounts, VAT, transaction and void counts, and sales by payment method. The text is a short summary of the same day. No report is sent for a day with no sales, voids or refunds.

Configure in **Settings > Automations > Report Delivery**.
