## Direction contract

THESIS: The marketing homepage stops pretending to be a soft consumer SaaS brochure and instead reads like the actual control surface the product is — proof of the multi-tenant/multi-branch architecture claim shown, not decorated; refuses the generic rounded-card-and-gradient-hero arrangement every POS landing page already uses.

OWN-WORLD: Pinned by explicit user brief, not rolled — Win8/Metro flat system from `app/globals.css`/`DESIGN.md`: zero border-radius anywhere (buttons, cards, badges, avatars), zero box-shadow, zero gradient; solid saturated color-fill tiles with white text and a `bg-white/15` icon chip; `hover:brightness-110` as the only interactive feedback; brand tokens `#35979c` teal (primary), `#2d868a` (hover), `#4aabae` (light), `#1e3a4c`/`#152a38` navy (chrome/section dividers); 1px `border-gray-300` hairlines in place of shadows for separation.

STORY: A Philippine SMB owner (single or multi-branch) or a multi-branch operator specifically evaluating cross-branch support lands here, immediately reads the product as a serious operational tool (not a toy), sees the multi-tenant/multi-branch architecture and BIR-compliance claims demonstrated as flat data/status tiles, and starts a 14-day free trial.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy (`#1e3a4c`) header bar, flat, no shadow. Hero section: left-aligned headline + subcopy + primary teal CTA button (square corners, `hover:brightness-110`), right side shows a flat "tile board" composition — solid-color status tiles (teal/navy/amber-equivalent-flat) standing in for branch/module counts, echoing the super-admin dashboard's tile language, not a screenshot mockup with rounded window chrome.

FORM: Extension of an established, brief-pinned visual world into a new surface (Persuade mode) — composition inherited from the existing page's proven structure (hero → stats band → feature tile grid → CTA band), materials fully replaced. No concept-seed roll applies: the roll's dealt structures (broadcast static, sticker album, kiln glaze, oscilloscope, Miura-fold) were assessed and rejected as incompatible with the pinned world and product category; seed key a8bd16eb, none locked.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
